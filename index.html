It’s just a conversation.

Open Rusty, call, and talk. About your day, your trip, whatever you covered in class. It remembers what you talked about last time and picks up from there.

Stuck on a word? Ask, and you get a nudge, not a lecture. No time to call? Send a voice note. No lessons, no streaks, no scores.

For people who know a little, or used to know a lot.

You don’t need perfect grammar. If you can put a few sentences together, even slowly, you have enough to start talking.

What helps next is saying it out loud, often, with someone who doesn’t mind waiting while you find the word. That’s how a language grows, and how it stays.
