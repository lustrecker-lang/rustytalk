Your account

You can try Rusty without an account; to keep your progress, sign in with Apple or Google. Keep your sign-in to yourself. You’re responsible for what happens in your account.

The AI teacher

Your teacher is an artificial intelligence, not a person. It can misunderstand you or make mistakes, in the language you are learning too. Don’t rely on it for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions, and never in an emergency. To give the teacher a voice and to understand what you say, your voice and the conversation are processed by AI services we work with (such as OpenAI and Google), only to run the app.

What you say

What you say and write in the app stays yours. You let us use it to run the app for you: to hold the conversation, track your progress and write your next lessons. If you send us feedback, we may use it to make Rusty better, and keep it after you delete your account, without your name or your voice.

Privacy

We keep what’s needed to teach you: your progress, what your teacher knows about you, and your calls as text. Your voice is not kept unless you switch on call recordings in the app’s Privacy settings. You can copy everything the app knows about you, and deleting your account removes all of it for good. The details are in our Privacy policy.

Be kind

Don’t use Rusty to harass anyone, for anything illegal, or to get the AI to produce harmful content. Don’t try to break, overload, copy or resell the app. If someone does, we may stop their account.

Paying

You can use Rusty for free, with a starter allowance of live-call minutes and your teacher’s voice notes and messages. Rusty Pro is a subscription (monthly or yearly) that gives you up to 30 minutes a day to call your teacher, up to three calls a day from your teacher and more voice notes; a free trial may be offered. Top-Up Packs add minutes once. Prices are shown before you buy, in your local currency. Payment, renewal, refunds and cancelling go through the App Store under Apple’s rules: a subscription renews until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings, at least a day before it renews. Minutes and packs are tied to your account and have no cash value.
